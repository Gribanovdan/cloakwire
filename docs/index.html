Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Windows or macOS show an unverified developer warning?

Cloakwire is 100% free and open-source without commercial monetization, so we do not purchase expensive enterprise certificates ($400+/yr). On Windows, click "More info" → "Run anyway". On macOS, right-click the app and choose "Open". All binaries are cryptographically signed with Minisign for in-app updates.

What protocols and subscription formats are supported?

Cloakwire supports VLESS (Reality, XTLS-Vision), VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria 2, TUIC, and WireGuard. It parses standard Base64 links, plaintext URL lists, Clash YAML configs, and custom JSON bundles.

Does Cloakwire collect any user data or logs?

Zero logs, zero telemetry, zero trackers. DNS queries are encrypted via DNS-over-HTTPS through the VPN tunnel, and all configuration parsing happens locally in the native Rust backend.
